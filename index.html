Integrative, Trauma-Informed Care

Evidence-Based Approaches, Tailored to You.

No two healing journeys look the same. Jennifer draws from seven established therapeutic approaches—blending them thoughtfully so therapy fits your nervous system, history, and comfort level.

You don't need to pick a method.

You don't have to know the difference between EMDR, IFS, or CBT before reaching out. Together, we decide what feels most supportive as we get to know your story.

Seven Core Modalities

How Each Approach Supports Healing.

01 Trauma Processing

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing

A structured, research-supported therapy that uses gentle bilateral stimulation to help the brain reprocess distressing memories so they feel less emotionally activating in the present.

02 Parts Work

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Compassionate Self-Leadership

Explores the different protective and vulnerable "parts" within you with curiosity rather than criticism—helping you build internal calm, clarity, and self-compassion.

03 Relational Roots

Post Induction Therapy (PIT)

Developmental Trauma & Boundaries

Addresses how early relational trauma shapes adult self-esteem, boundaries, and relationship dynamics—supporting healthier emotional moderation and connection.

04 Emotional Healing

Inner Child Work

Tending Unmet Early Needs

A gentle process of recognizing younger emotional wounds that still echo in adulthood, offering them safety, validation, and nurturing from your grounded adult self.

05 Practical Skills

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Thoughts, Feelings & Habits

Helps identify unhelpful thought patterns and everyday stressors, building practical coping strategies to reduce anxiety, low mood, and emotional overwhelm.

06 Present Awareness

Gestalt Therapy

Here-and-Now Connection

Focuses on present-moment awareness—noticing how emotions show up in your body, thoughts, and relationships right now so you can respond with greater choice.

07 Empowering Change

Motivational Interviewing

Respecting Readiness & Autonomy

A collaborative, non-judgmental conversation style especially helpful during recovery or major life transitions. It honors your ambivalence, clarifies your own values, and strengthens your confidence to make meaningful change at your own pace.

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

Sunrise Counseling, LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Website Communication: Standard email and website inquiry forms are not encrypted clinical communication channels. Please avoid submitting sensitive health, diagnostic, or clinical information via email or web forms. All clinical records, intake paperwork, and virtual therapy sessions are conducted through our secure, HIPAA-compliant client portal (jennifer-connelly.clientsecure.me).

Confidentiality in Psychotherapy: Information shared within psychotherapy sessions is confidential in accordance with federal HIPAA regulations and applicable South Carolina and Florida state laws, subject to standard legal exceptions (such as imminent risk of harm to self or others, or mandated reporting of abuse/neglect). Full Notice of Privacy Practices documentation is provided prior to beginning treatment.

Professional Disclosures

Clinician & Licensure: Services at Sunrise Counseling, LLC are provided by Jennifer Connelly, MA, Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC, Florida) and Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC, South Carolina).

Telehealth Jurisdiction: Virtual psychotherapy sessions are available exclusively to clients physically located within the states of South Carolina or Florida at the time of service.

Good Faith Estimate & Insurance: In accordance with the No Surprises Act, self-pay clients have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges. Insurance acceptance through Headway depends on individual plan eligibility and benefits verification.

Not an Emergency Service: Sunrise Counseling, LLC does not provide 24/7 crisis intervention. If you are experiencing an urgent mental health crisis, please call or text 988, dial 911, or visit your nearest emergency department.
